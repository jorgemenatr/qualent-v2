Logistics Firm Hires Fourth Dispatcher to Manage Spreadsheet That Tracks the Other Three
 
Hamilton, Ont. — By Gazette staff
 
The Llama in the dispatch office, before it opened. Staff do not know how. — Gazette photo
 
A regional freight company confirmed Tuesday that its newest dispatcher will be responsible for maintaining the spreadsheet that tracks what the other three dispatchers are doing, a role management described as “critical.” The workbook, which reportedly contains 41 tabs and one macro nobody will touch, is opened by all four employees simultaneously each morning, at which point it asks whether they want to save changes.

The Pickle Llama, who was observed in the dispatch office before it opened, declined to comment on the hire. Asked whether the spreadsheet could be replaced, he said, “Yes.” He then left.
