## The Problem

High-volume manufacturing hiring in Mexico's maquiladora sector faces brutal economics. Monthly turnover runs at 9.37%, which means a facility with 1,000 workers needs to hire roughly 94 replacements every single month just to stay at capacity. The average time-to-hire sits at 55 days. Recruiters handle about 28 hires per month each, and cost per hire ranges from $450 to $1,290 USD depending on the position.

The process is almost entirely manual. Recruiters spend their days fielding WhatsApp messages from candidates — the dominant communication channel in Mexico — copying information between chat windows and spreadsheets, chasing down identity documents, and scheduling interviews that 25% of candidates simply don't show up for. The repetitive, low-value work consumes the time that should go toward evaluating candidates and building talent pipelines.

## The Solution

We built a WhatsApp-native AI recruitment platform that automates the three most time-consuming stages of the hiring process: initial screening, document verification, and interview scheduling.

### Conversational Screening

The AI bot conducts initial candidate screening entirely through WhatsApp, communicating in conversational Mexican Spanish. It asks qualifying questions about experience, availability, shift preferences, and transportation — adapting its conversation flow based on the specific role requirements. Candidates who meet the baseline criteria advance automatically; those who don't receive a respectful explanation.

The bot responds in under 30 seconds, operates around the clock, and handles unlimited concurrent conversations. Recruiters no longer spend hours on initial screening calls that yield a low conversion rate.

### Document Verification

When candidates need to submit identity documents — INE (voter ID), CURP (national registry), proof of address — the system accepts photos directly through WhatsApp. OCR processing extracts and validates the document data, checking for completeness, legibility, and consistency. Documents that pass verification are automatically filed; those with issues trigger a specific, helpful request back to the candidate explaining exactly what needs to be resubmitted.

This eliminates the back-and-forth that previously consumed significant recruiter time, particularly when candidates submitted blurry photos or incomplete documents.

### Automated Interview Scheduling

Qualified candidates with verified documents are offered available interview slots directly in the WhatsApp conversation. The system integrates with Google Calendar and Outlook to check recruiter availability in real-time. Once a candidate confirms, both parties receive calendar invitations.

A reminder system sends notifications at 24 hours and 2 hours before the interview, with an easy rescheduling option. This targeted approach directly addresses the no-show problem that wastes recruiter time and extends time-to-hire.

## The Results

The platform delivers measurable improvements across every key recruitment metric:

- **Cost per hire reduced 40-45%** — from $450-$1,290 down to $250-$710, driven primarily by automation of screening and document processing
- **Time-to-hire cut from 55 days to 25-35 days** — faster screening and document verification eliminate the bottlenecks that stretch the process
- **Recruiter capacity increased from 28 to 70-100 hires per month** — a 3x improvement, allowing existing teams to handle growth without proportional headcount increases
- **No-show rate reduced from 25% to under 15%** — automated reminders and easy rescheduling keep candidates engaged through the final stage

For a facility hiring 94 workers per month, the cost savings alone represent tens of thousands of dollars annually. But the capacity increase is arguably more valuable — it means the existing recruitment team can support facility expansion without scaling linearly.

## Why It Works

The key insight is meeting candidates where they already are. WhatsApp is not an alternative channel in Mexico — it is the primary channel. Building the entire recruitment flow natively within WhatsApp eliminated the friction of asking candidates to download apps, visit portals, or check email. The result is higher completion rates at every stage of the funnel.
