## The Project

Tecnomundo is a technology retailer based in Merida, Mexico, offering consumer electronics, computing equipment, and accessories to both retail and business customers. Their existing web presence had fallen behind — slow to load, difficult to navigate on mobile, and not representative of the quality of service they deliver in-store.

We redesigned and rebuilt the site from the ground up using Next.js and Tailwind CSS, focusing on fast load times, clean product presentation, and a mobile-first experience that reflects how the majority of their customers browse. The new design emphasizes clarity and ease of navigation, making it simple for visitors to find products, understand pricing, and get in touch.

## What Comes Next

The website is the starting point, not the destination. Our engagement with Tecnomundo is focused on understanding their full operation — from inventory management and supplier relationships to in-store workflows and customer service — to identify where technology can solve the highest-cost problems.

We're currently exploring opportunities across their supply chain and retail operations, with the goal of finding the interventions that deliver the most measurable impact. The website establishes the digital foundation; the real value will come from the operational improvements we build on top of it.

This is how we prefer to work — start with a concrete deliverable, build trust through execution, and then go deeper into the problems that matter most.
