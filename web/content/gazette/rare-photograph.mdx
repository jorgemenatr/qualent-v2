The Gazette has obtained a photograph understood to show the Pickle Llama. It is reproduced here at the original grain.

Nothing about the image is established. The location is unknown. The date is unknown. The photographer is unknown and has not come forward. Two readers have written in to say they recognise the wall behind him; they do not agree on which wall it is.

The Llama has declined to confirm or deny that the photograph shows him, and declined to discuss what he was before the image was made.

The Gazette notes only that the image predates every documented sighting, and that in it he is chewing.

Readers with information about the photograph are asked to write to the editor. Readers with information about the wall are asked not to.
